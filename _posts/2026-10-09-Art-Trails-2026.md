The annual <a href="https://www.sonomacountyarttrails.org/">Sonoma County Art Trails</a> begins this weekend and is my first at the new location, here on the river in central Petaluma.

The weather looks fine, and as long as it doesn't get windy, my corner of the event (out of over 100 corners across the county) will be held outdoors. Watch for the signs and come through on the side of the building with the small open gate. Plenty of free and convenient parking, sunshine, prints and books, what better weekend?
